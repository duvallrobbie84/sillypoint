A CRICKET SCORING COMPANION
What is it?
Silly Point is a lightweight, no-fuss scoring companion for casual and friendly cricket. Designed to be used on the boundary, in the field, or tucked in your back pocket. Open it, score a match, export the numbers. That's it.
What is it not?
A full scoring app. Silly Point doesn't aim to displace PlayCricket or other excellent platforms. It won't produce a full scorecard, track individual batters, or log bowling figures. It's intentionally simple — a super lightweight web app that works with no setup, no login, and no fuss.
